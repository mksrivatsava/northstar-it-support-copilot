# Northstar Retail · Collaboration support

## Teams audio and microphone troubleshooting

If Microsoft Teams has no audio or your microphone is not working, open Teams Settings, then Devices, and select the correct speaker and microphone. Check the operating system microphone permissions and make a test call. Disconnect and reconnect your headset. Restart Teams after changing devices. If the problem continues, include your operating system, headset model, and Teams version in a service-desk ticket.

## Teams application problems after an update

If the Teams application is acting strange after an update, save your work, quit Teams completely, and restart it. Restart the laptop if needed and verify you are using the company-approved version from Software Center. Do not delete application data or install older versions without IT guidance. Report the error message, version, and steps to reproduce to the service desk.

## Suspected collaboration service outage

If multiple colleagues cannot access Teams, record the start time and affected office locations and notify the service desk. Use company email for urgent coordination if it is available. This runbook describes the response procedure; it does not confirm whether a vendor outage is happening. The service desk verifies vendor status before communicating an incident update.
