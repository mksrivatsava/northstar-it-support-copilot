# Northstar Retail · Office connectivity

## Office wireless network

The employee wireless network is Northstar-Corp. Connect using your managed laptop and corporate account. The guest network is Northstar-Guest and cannot access internal systems. If the wireless connection drops, disable and enable the wireless adapter, reconnect to Northstar-Corp, and restart the laptop if needed. If other employees are affected, report the office location and time of failure to the service desk.

## Internal application connection errors

If an internal application cannot be reached from home, confirm the VPN client shows Connected and that your internet connection works. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN once. Record the application name, error message, and timestamp. Do not change firewall rules or disable endpoint protection. Escalate repeated failures to the service desk.
