# Northstar Retail · Restricted operations runbook

## Break-glass account access review

The break-glass account review is restricted to authorized IT administrators. The on-call security lead must approve every use in the incident record. Two administrators review the access log after the incident. Credentials are held in the corporate vault and are never stored in this knowledge base. Rotate the emergency credential after each authorized use. Employees must contact the service desk rather than attempt emergency administrator access.

## Remote wipe approval

Only the security lead may approve a remote wipe after verifying device ownership and reviewing the incident record. An authorized IT administrator executes the wipe through the managed endpoint platform. Record approval, device identifier, executor, and completion time in the incident record.
