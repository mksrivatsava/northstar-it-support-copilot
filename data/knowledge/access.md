# Northstar Retail · Identity and access

## VPN connection from home

To connect to the company VPN from home, first use a company-managed laptop with an internet connection. Open the Northstar VPN client and select the Corporate gateway. Sign in using your work email and password, then approve the MFA notification on your registered device. Confirm the client displays Connected before opening internal applications. If the VPN fails, record the error code and contact the service desk. Never install a VPN client from an unapproved download site.

## Password reset and account lockout

Reset a forgotten password using the Self-Service Password Reset link on the company sign-in screen. Verify your identity using the registered recovery method, choose a unique passphrase of at least 14 characters, and sign in again. If your account is locked, wait 15 minutes before retrying. If your recovery device is unavailable, contact the service desk for identity verification. Never send a password in a ticket or chat.

## MFA codes and lost phones

IT support will never ask you to share a password, MFA code, recovery code, or authentication approval. Do not share MFA codes with anyone, including support personnel. Deny unexpected MFA prompts and report them to the security team. If you lose your phone or cannot access MFA, contact the service desk for identity verification and MFA re-enrollment. Security controls must never be bypassed.
