# Northstar Retail · Service desk guide

## Opening a support ticket

Create a service-desk ticket with a short description, the affected application or device, the exact error message, when the issue started, and the steps already tried. Include your device asset tag when relevant. Do not include passwords, MFA codes, payment card data, or customer personal information. This local copilot stores demo tickets only; it does not send them to Jira, ServiceNow, or an external support team.

## Incident priority and response targets

A widespread store checkout outage is priority P1 and should be escalated immediately to the on-call incident manager. An issue blocking one employee with no workaround is P2 with a target initial response within four business hours. A general information or software request is P3 with a target initial response within two business days. These are fictional demonstration targets, not measured service guarantees.
